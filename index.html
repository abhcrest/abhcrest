About the Firm

Institutional Depth.
Strategic Agility.

ABH Crest Ltd is an Abuja-based multi-disciplinary consulting firm operating at the intersection of public policy, private commerce, and institutional governance. We deliver strategic advisory, technology implementation, project management, and trade facilitation services to government agencies, parastatals, international organisations, and corporate entities across Nigeria and Africa.

Our principal brings a rare combination of International Relations expertise and Information & Communications Technology — enabling the firm to navigate the most complex multi-stakeholder environments with both diplomatic sensitivity and technical precision.

From managing R&A-sanctioned continental championships to delivering enterprise ICT systems for federal regulatory bodies, ABH Crest consistently operates at the highest institutional levels.

Project Management & Event Consulting

02
ICT Solutions & Systems Implementation
Customisation and deployment of enterprise software systems for government and corporate clients — including legal and case management platforms, workflow automation, and digital infrastructure. Delivered under formal procurement and parastatal tender frameworks.
03
Strategic & Institutional Advisory
High-level advisory to organisations navigating complex policy environments, inter-agency dynamics, and institutional reform. We develop strategic frameworks, position papers, white papers, and provide executive-level counsel on sensitive institutional matters.

Sourcing & Manufacturer Representation
Acting as authorised sourcing agents and manufacturers’ representatives for a broad range of goods — technology hardware, industrial equipment, consumer products, and specialised institutional items from China, UAE, Europe, and the Americas.
